# Character export without an addon - part 2 of 2 (race + gear: color, level, icon).
# Same way: NEW macro in game, paste the line below and press the MACRO button (not chat!).
# WEXP0|... and WEXP2|... lines appear in chat - copy ALL of them to the Hero page.
